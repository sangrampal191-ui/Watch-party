🎬 Watch Party

1. Join Party

Connect to a room first.

Both friends must enter the same Room ID and have an internet connection.

2. Watch Video

You can paste a YouTube link or a direct MP4/WebM video link. The website will automatically choose the correct player. Some YouTube videos may not allow embedding.

3. Live Chat 💬

How to use

1. Open this website on both phones.
2. Enter the same Room ID and connect.
3. Paste a YouTube link or direct video link.
4. Use Play, Pause and Seek to sync.
5. Use chat to talk with your friend.
